# 📊 UniHub Connect - Financial Report (February 2026)

This document summarizes the pricing and commission structure used by UniHub Connect as of February 2026. It is intended for internal review and to serve as a reference for revenue calculations and platform economics.

---

## 1. Payment Gateway Costs (Paymob Standard)

- **Fee structure:** 2.75% of transaction value + 3 EGP per transaction.
- **Service fee policy:** UniHub adds a service fee on top of the product price to the customer. It covers the Paymob costs and includes a markup for the platform.

### Student Pro details

- **Monthly subscription:** 150 EGP, introductory 99 EGP for first 99 users.
- **Per-sale credit:** 1 EGP deducted from the subscription balance for each order.

## 3. Variable Commissions (Big Brand categories)

Commission rate depends on the order value:

| Order value (EGP)    | Commission rate |
|----------------------|-----------------|
| Up to 500            | 6%              |
| 501 – 3,000          | 5%              |
| Above 3,000          | 2.5%            |

## 4. Example Transaction

A product priced at 300 EGP sold by a Student Pro vendor:

1. **Customer pays:** 315 EGP (300 + 15 service fee).
2. **Paymob fee:** 11.25 EGP (2.75% of 300 + 3).
3. **Platform commission:** 15 EGP (5% of 300).
4. **Subscription credit:** 1 EGP deducted from Student Pro balance.
5. **Platform net revenue:** 19.75 EGP (commission + leftover service fee minus Paymob fee).
6. **Amount to vendor:** 284 EGP.

> **Note:** service fee markup is retained by UniHub to cover gateway cost and profit.

## 5. General Policies

- **Automated deductions:** Subscriptions and commissions are automatically deducted from the seller's wallet balance.
- **Withdrawal fee:** A fixed 5 EGP fee applies when sellers transfer earnings from the wallet to their bank or mobile wallet.
